About

Where payments strategy meets product and AI.

Lily is a product strategist at JPMorgan Chase, where she sits at the intersection of payments, technology, and executive decision-making. Across four rotations inside one of the world's largest financial institutions, she has directed go-to-market strategy and multi-million-dollar portfolio planning for omnichannel and biometric payment products.

She currently leads the firm's evaluation and rollout of AI-powered workflow tools — work that cut manual processing time by 30% and sharpened data accuracy across her team. Her background spans product roadmapping, competitive and market analysis, and translating complex data into decisions executive leadership can act on.

“I build the case that gets a product prioritized — from market sizing and competitive analysis to the board deck that gets it funded.” Product Strategy Associate, JPMorgan Chase
